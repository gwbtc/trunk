Trunk

Calls, party lines and notifications for your ship

Notifications

Your ship sends these to the phones and computers below. Tlon decides which messages are worth a notification. These switches narrow that, and Talon's setting for each chat or group applies on top. A channel with no setting in Talon, for itself or its group, notifies you only of mentions and of replies in your threads.

Apps that send alerts

Each app on this ship that has sent an alert, with its own switch. A new app is let through until you switch it off. Each app gets at most one alert every 5 seconds, and alerts that come faster are combined into one. An app may have 30 in an hour unless you change its limit here. Trunk tells your phone which app each alert came from.
